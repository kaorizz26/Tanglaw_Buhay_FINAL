New import

Upload CBMS source files

Select the official survey year and the four required CSV files. The system supplies the approved codebook, indicator mapping and field definitions.

4 CSV files required

Your active dataset will not change. A passing bundle is stored as inactive until its assessments are prepared and an administrator activates it.

CBMS files

Select the survey year, then use the exact filenames shown below.

household.csv

Main household-level and dwelling records.

section_a_e_indiv.csv

Individual member records from survey Sections A–E.

section_l02_to_l41.csv

Health responses from survey Sections L02–L41.

section_l11_to_l14.csv

Child mortality responses from survey Sections L11–L14.

Upload limit

All four files together must be under 256 MiB. Checking and storage may take a few minutes.
