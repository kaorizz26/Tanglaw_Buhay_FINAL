How are priorities determined?

The system considers household vulnerability and recorded conditions relevant to assistance programs.

  • Primary Priority: a vulnerability score above 25 and at least one subsidy For Review finding.
  • Secondary Priority: a vulnerability score from 20 to 25, including both boundaries, and at least one subsidy For Review finding.

These groups help staff decide which households to examine first. They do not confirm official poverty classification, subsidy eligibility or approval. Households outside the priority groups can still be examined.
