# Tanglaw-Buhay UI/UX specification

## Purpose and authority

Tanglaw-Buhay helps authorized LGU staff understand historical household conditions, automated vulnerability assessments, and separate subsidy screening findings. It supports decisions; it does not issue official poverty classifications, determine eligibility, approve benefits, or replace staff judgment.

This document sets the current staff-facing UI direction and responsive expectations for a future citizen portal. [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) holds permanent development, security, and data rules; [PROJECT_CONTEXT.md](PROJECT_CONTEXT.md) explains the system and approved assessment boundaries. The [implementation plan](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) and [Developer Guide](../DEVELOPER_GUIDE.md) retain task history and technical detail, but historical screen arrangements and testing records are not current UI requirements. Follow the current approved task within the permanent rules.

## General experience

- Design for LGU personnel who have not read the CBMS questionnaire or developer documentation. Use plain, precise labels and explain what a finding means for the household.
- Make the next relevant action and related information easy to find. Use a clear visual hierarchy: essential household information and findings first, supporting evidence on demand. Preserve existing branding while allowing layouts and components to improve when an approved task calls for it.
- Make available and planned destinations clear in navigation. LGU Staff see Dashboard, Household Records, Assessments, and planned Submissions. System Administrators see Dashboard, Household Records, Assessments, Data Import, and Staff Accounts. Data Import remains a protected placeholder; Staff Accounts currently supports account creation only.
- Staff and administrators use the same sign-in form. New LGU Staff accounts are created by the administrator and must set their own password after signing in with the initial one; there is no self-registration.
- Keep pages readable and accessible at the viewports appropriate to each portal, with keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and meaningful labels. Disclosures must open and close predictably without obstructing other controls.
- Show uncertainty where it matters, briefly and accurately. Never invent missing answers or imply that supplemental or derived values were directly collected or verified by PSA.
- Keep routine screens free of synthetic-support labels, pilot-development or provenance narratives, technical formulas, data-preparation notes, internal task references, developer assumptions, and lengthy methodology. Retain those details in internal metadata and technical documentation.
- Check usability from a first-time staff perspective as well as functional correctness before completing a screen.
- Present information as grouped, scannable sections, not article-like text. Use navy, white and gold accents, consistent borders and spacing, and only the cards, colors and statistics that carry meaning. Show a fixed context value, such as the active survey year, as clearly read-only rather than as a control that appears changeable.

## Responsive design by portal

### LGU staff portal

- Design primarily for desktop and laptop browsers. Use **1440px** as the primary design and testing viewport, with quick compatibility checks at **1366px** and **1920px**.
- Prioritize readable tables, efficient navigation, accessible controls, and professional desktop workflows. Keep important information and actions easy to scan and reach.
- Maintain basic responsiveness at smaller widths: layouts must not break, the page must not overflow horizontally, and functionality must remain accessible. Wide data tables may scroll within their own containers when necessary.
- Routine staff portal tasks do not require extensive mobile-specific optimization or testing. Check smaller widths when the affected layout or interaction warrants it.

### Citizen portal (future implementation)

- Design mobile-first for citizens using smartphones, tablets, laptops, or desktop computers. Provide a complete, usable experience across screen sizes.
- Keep forms, document uploads, navigation, and other citizen features accessible on both mobile and desktop.
- When the citizen portal is implemented, perform appropriate mobile and desktop usability testing for its affected workflows. Its access and security rules require an approved implementation task.

## Staff modules

| Module | Staff purpose and scope |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | Provide a clear entry point to available staff work and relevant system information. Add summary content only when its meaning and source are defined. |
| Household Records | Help authorized staff find and understand household and member records, with enough context to interpret answers. Define its navigation and any permitted staff actions in the approved implementation task; preserve original source data. |
| Assessments — Household Prioritization | Find households by their stored vulnerability results using the existing search, filters, and pagination. Explain the score and municipal rank in everyday language; these controls must not change the rank calculated from the full selected source batch. |
| Assessments — Household Assessment | Explain a household's Overview and MPI Indicators, with supporting evidence available when needed. Show the four dimensions and all 16 indicators without changing stored calculations or outcomes. |
| Subsidy Screening | Present separate historical findings for 4Ps, SLP, SPISC, and TUPAD with understandable reasons and focused recorded information. Use the staff-facing labels For Review, Not Enough Information, and No Review Signal while preserving stored statuses. Screening results are decision support, not an approval, eligibility decision, or opened staff review. |
| Staff Reviews | LGU Staff can start and open a program review, append attributed notes, finish with a recommendation and explanation, or cancel without a recommendation. Preserve read-only history and keep screening findings, review status, and recommendations distinct. |
| Review Queue | Municipality-wide staff work under In Review, For LGU Follow-Up, and No Further Action. Show one current review per household/program, with direct workspace access. Cancellations stay in history and cannot remove an effective completed recommendation. |
| Submissions | When implemented, provide a clear place for the approved staff submission workflow. Define its actions and permissions in the task that implements it. |
| Administration | Let System Admins manage approved import and staff-account functions. Keep these actions admin-only and behind the existing source-access controls. |

The table states intended purposes, not a claim that every module is already implemented. Consult the relevant implementation-plan section for current milestone status before changing a module. It does not prescribe specific cards, metric rows, bars, or component arrangements.

## Assessment content and evidence

- Make the household identity, historical survey context, score, municipal rank, and assessment findings understandable without requiring methodology knowledge. The score reflects recorded deprivation in the existing MPI; do not introduce new vulnerability categories or recompute results in the UI.
- On the Overview, explain what the household's results mean across the four MPI dimensions. Let staff locate indicators needing attention or those with unknown findings in the relevant dimension.
- For each of the 16 MPI indicators, present its name, Deprived, Not Deprived, or Unknown status, a concise explanation of the finding, its points contribution, and View Evidence for relevant household or member answers. An explanation should add meaning, not merely repeat the status.
- Evidence should identify the relevant household or member and show understandable recorded answers. Include enough original survey context—such as population, reason, period, or units—when a shortened field label would change the answer's meaning. Decode known values accurately; describe genuinely missing, conflicting, or uncertain responses briefly.
- CBMS Record Details was intentionally removed from MPI Indicators. Preserve original source values and mappings internally; keep staff-facing View Evidence understandable without technical record details.
- Keep the four subsidy programs' automated findings separate from MPI and from any later staff review. Do not add eligibility rules, change screening engines, or present a system flag as a staff decision.
- Use system statuses precisely: MPI indicator findings are **Deprived**, **Not Deprived**, or **Unknown**. For routine subsidy screens, display stored `For Review` as **For Review**, `Needs Data Review` as **Not Enough Information**, and `No Historical CBMS Trigger` as **No Review Signal**. These labels are separate from staff review decisions and do not automatically open a review.
- SPISC uses one current-age recommendation across Household Assessment, Overview, Household Prioritization filters/counts and Staff Review initiation. Show recorded survey age and dated estimated age in the existing member cards, retaining uncertainty and attributable disability/PWD support. Keep the unchanged historical result in View Recorded Information. Other programs retain stored findings; MPI scores and municipal ranks remain unchanged. SPISC For Review needs no additional starting reason; the other two statuses require one, revalidated by the server.
- Staff Reviews replaces the planned Review History tab. Start Review opens a short household/program confirmation form. Require a brief reason for Not Enough Information or No Review Signal; do not ask for an extra justification for For Review. An active review has no duplicate Start Review or unfinished Continue Review action. Starting a review does not imply eligibility, contact, document submission, or approval. Start Review and the review workspace show a compact Screening Findings & Information to Check panel from the stored screening guidance; on closed or cancelled reviews it is collapsed and labelled as current reference, not saved with the review.
- Open Review presents the saved start details and chronological notes. Show one small note form; Finish Review and Cancel Review open separate short forms. Finishing requires **For LGU Follow-Up** or **No Further Action** and an explanation. Cancellation makes no recommendation. Show Start Review only when no active review or effective completed recommendation exists for that program. An effective completed recommendation has a clear View Review action; staff use correction or withdrawal instead of starting a repeat. Direct and stale start submissions follow the same rule and guide staff to View Review.
- Only the latest completed, unwithdrawn recommendation offers **Correct Recommendation** (primary) and **Withdraw Recommendation** (secondary) in the focused workspace. Forms use the wider main area and retain the current recommendation, explanation and attribution. Correction requires an intentional category choice, a reason (1,000 characters), and an updated explanation (2,000); explanation-only corrections must actually change the explanation. Review Correction shows previous/new recommendations, reason and explanation; only Confirm and Save writes. Back to Edit retains entries; Cancel returns without saving.
- Withdrawal requires a reason (1,000 characters) and a separate confirmation identifying the household, subsidy and saved recommendation. Keep Current Recommendation returns without saving. Show **Recommendation Withdrawn** on household cards, Overview and the focused record; remove it from completed queue lists. Preserve original and changed decisions in chronological, attributed recommendation history, including previous/new explanations and reasons. Older/superseded records remain read-only; withdrawal never restores an older recommendation. Withdrawal makes Start Review available when no active review exists.
- Show Staff Review Status only inside Overview, above Areas Contributing to Vulnerability in the wider left column. Keep Subsidy Screening Summary at the top of the narrower right column; stack these panels in that order on smaller screens. Show a compact No Staff Review Started state when no reviews exist, otherwise each reviewed program's current state or effective recommendation. Individual program cards retain the same statuses on Staff Reviews.
- Each Overview panel has a navy-blue navigation link with white text: View Staff Reviews →, View MPI Indicators →, or View Subsidy Screening →. View Staff Reviews uses a URL fragment to reveal its heading and program cards directly. All assessment/review navigation preserves validated return filters; do not duplicate evidence or show internal review identifiers.

## Review Queue

- The expandable Assessments sidebar provides Household Prioritization and Review Queue, with the relevant child highlighted and submenu open on assessment/review pages. Do not duplicate those links as page-top tabs. Use one row of three compact clickable status cards: In Review (default), For LGU Follow-Up, and No Further Action. Each card combines its label and meaning; do not add a separate tab row or explanation panel. Use navy/gold selection, visible Selected text, accessible links with aria-current, and clear hover/focus states. Show three equal columns at desktop widths and stack at narrow widths. Use “Staff have started these reviews but have not finished them yet.”; “Staff have finished these reviews and recommend further consideration by the LGU.”; and “Staff have finished these reviews and recommend no additional action at this time.” Add once: “Staff recommendations do not mean subsidy approval or denial of eligibility.” Automated screening findings never create queue rows.
- Show Municipal Rank, Household ID, Household Head, Barangay, Subsidy, Staff Review, Date Started/Finished, and Action. Ranks come unchanged from the designated completed run. Ongoing reviews are oldest first; finished reviews are most recently finished first. Derive one current row per household/program consistently across queue, cards and Overview: preserve any existing active review; otherwise show the latest completed review including its corrections/withdrawal; only use cancellation as the current status if no completion exists. A newer cancellation cannot erase an earlier completed recommendation. A withdrawn latest completion blocks all older recommendations. Legacy active repeats keep their existing workflow; while active, Open Review takes precedence. Cancelling such a repeat reveals the preserved effective completion; finishing it replaces the earlier completion. Completed correction actions remain unavailable while another active review takes precedence.
- Below the status explanation and above filters, show a compact Assigned to Me / All Staff Reviews segmented control with one short contextual explanation. Default to Assigned to Me when scope is omitted. Assigned to Me uses current Responsible Staff, including for completed records. Switching scope preserves other selections and resets page; preserve scope through filters, status tabs, pagination and review returns. Distinguish an empty personal list from unmatched search/filters, with a short link to All Staff Reviews.
- Search by household reference or head name; filter by barangay and any selected subsidy programs. Offer ascending/descending Sort by Date Started or Finished, retaining existing default orders. Apply Filters submits all selections and resets page; Clear Filters resets search, barangay, subsidy and date selections while retaining status and scope. Use 25 reviews per page and a contained scrollable table at narrow widths. Status links preserve applied selections and reset page.
- Keep one row per household/program. Show Responsible Staff under the row's review status, mark the signed-in staff member with “(you)”, and briefly explain that other authorized staff can help. Do not add a ninth column.
- Open Review and View Review go directly to the existing focused record. Queue-origin navigation provides compact individually bordered Back to Review Queue, View Staff Reviews, View Household Assessment, and View Subsidy Screening controls. View Staff Reviews opens the existing four-program household tab at its heading. Preserve validated status, staff selection, date order, search, barangay, subsidies, and page. Household-origin reviews retain Back to Staff Reviews, and Prioritization-origin assessments retain Back to Prioritization.
- Starting a review assigns its starter as Responsible Staff for that subsidy review. Preserve Started By separately. Authorized staff can reassign an active review using an active-staff selector, required brief reason, and Confirm Reassignment. Show who changed responsibility, from/to staff, time, and reason in assignment history. Reject stale changes and keep closed/cancelled records read-only; responsibility never restricts existing shared note/finish/cancel access.
- After finishing, show the saved record and name its recommendation tab. It leaves In Review; cancellation preserves history without adding a queue row. Corrections move the latest effective recommendation to its matching existing tab; withdrawal adds no new tab. Preserve all validated queue return state throughout editing, confirmation, saving and cancellation.

## Design and change boundaries

Reuse shared branding, navigation, styles, and accessible interaction patterns where they serve staff well. An implemented layout is not a permanent requirement solely because it appears in an earlier task description. Change only the screen and behavior approved for the current task, preserve assessment and security rules, and verify that staff can understand the result.

Earlier versions of this file remain in Git as a record of frontend tasks, testing, the [historical Figma reference](https://www.figma.com/design/bDnkjYWFZW7cSmROuazJSp/Tanglaw-Buhay-UI?node-id=22-907), and a proposed future citizen portal. Those records do not authorize or prescribe current staff UI work; future citizen access and workflows need their own approved scope and security rules.
